# Patient Care Advocates (PCA Tucson) — About Us

Official responsive website for **Patient Care Advocates (PCA)**, providing in-home healthcare, Medicare nursing, caregiver services, and bedside hospital advocacy in Tucson and Southern Arizona.

## Features
- **Corporate & Compassionate Design**: Tailored Tailwind CSS palette with Tucson Navy, Heritage Burgundy, and Warm Gold
- **Leadership Spotlight**: Profiles of Founder Alice A. Milton, Esq., Executive Director Olivia Cortinas-Romero, and Director of Nursing Wendy Owen, RN
- **Rotary Four-Way Test**: Integration of core ethical healthcare standards
- **Broadcast Media Embed**: Responsive video player for the KGUN 9 Tucson Morning Blend special report
- **Hospital Advocacy Risk Guide**: Real statistics, patient safety facts, and advocacy protections
- **Verified Client Testimonials**: Letters from Tucson families, adult children, and medical social workers
- **Interactive Intake Modal & Contact**: 24/7 care assessment request form with phone dispatch links
