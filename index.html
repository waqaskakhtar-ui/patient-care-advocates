Critical Patient Safety Information

Hospital Stays Can Be Hazardous Without an Advocate at Your Bedside.

Problems in today’s modern hospitals have been thoroughly documented in national investigations. The alarming reality is that countless patients entering hospitals suffer avoidable setbacks or find themselves alone, overwhelmed, and unable to ask the right medical questions.

"…for every 100 patients admitted to hospitals, there are 40 to 50 incidents in which patients are harmed… ranging from bruises and bed sores to much more life-threatening situations."
— CBS News Special Report

The Hard Facts in Healthcare

  • Hospital-acquired infections: The risk of serious antibiotic-resistant infection during admission continues to climb.
  • Forced immobility: Days in hospital beds cause muscle atrophy that may prevent seniors from ever returning home independently.
  • Physician communication lag: It can take days before relatives receive an honest, plain-English assessment from attending physicians.
  • Medication errors at discharge: Mismatched prescriptions between hospital and home are a leading cause of readmission.
